## Direction

- **Concept:** The six minutes after a meeting, in one take: the transcript runs out, nobody has notes, and the product writes them while we watch.
- **Tone:** Dry and observational, like a documentary with subtitles; the film never raises its voice.
- **Palette:** The product's own warm white fills the frame; a paper-coloured band at the bottom carries the words, and green appears only where something is decided or done.
- **Typography:** Outfit, large and plain, one short line at a time in a band along the bottom, like subtitles; nothing is ever set beside or over the product.
- **Motion:** The product is the whole picture and the camera travels across it: down the transcript, sideways to the notes, down the notes as they are written. It never pushes in for effect and never leaves the page until the end.
- **Pacing:** Slow at the start, while the meeting runs out; one click; then each thing the product writes gets its own unhurried beat. The film ends when the notes are sent, not on a slogan.
