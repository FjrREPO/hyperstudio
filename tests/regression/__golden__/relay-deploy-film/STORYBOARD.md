## Direction

- **Concept:** One line of code travels from an editor to every machine in production, and each stop on the way is a different picture.
- **Tone:** Terse and precise, like good tooling; nothing is explained twice and nothing is decorated.
- **Palette:** The product's own near-black is the whole frame; amber marks what is being changed, green is the new release, and the fleet turning green is the film's one colour event.
- **Typography:** None of the film's own until the name at the end: every word before it is code, a log or the product, in its monospace, set large enough to read.
- **Motion:** What moves is the deploy itself, a line typed, a bar filling, shards finishing, machines turning; the camera only steps back to show how far it has got.
- **Pacing:** Four milestones of about six seconds, each cut on its completion, with the longest hold on the fleet fully turned before the name.
