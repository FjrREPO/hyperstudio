## Direction

- **Concept:** One deploy, watched from inside the machine: a push, a build, a canary, a promotion, and an undo, with no narrator on screen.
- **Tone:** Terse and precise, like good tooling; nothing is explained twice and nothing is decorated.
- **Palette:** The product's own near-black is the whole frame, with its green for what is healthy, amber for what is in flight, and a black void where the camera sees past its edge.
- **Typography:** None of the film's own: every word on screen is the product's, in its monospace; the story is told by the log and by a voice.
- **Motion:** The interface is an object the camera circles on a wide lens, a new angle for each stage of the deploy, coming square only when the release is live.
- **Pacing:** Fast and even while the pipeline runs, one still breath when it is live, then the undo, and a slow pull back on the command that starts it all.
