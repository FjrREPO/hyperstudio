# Review: Lumen - 17 second product showcase

Answered from frames on 2026-11-19: the 20 review snapshots (two per shot), a 30-frame sheet and two full
frames pulled from the rendered file. Sound was measured, not heard. A passing check is not a passing review.

- HyperFrames check: 0 errors, 0 warnings
- Rendered file: 17.0 s, video and audio; peak -2.0 dB, mean -22.2 dB
- Placeholders: the brand (Lumen is an example product), all four screens, the brand mark, the sound effects,
  and all five narration lines (the operating system's voice, a draft)

## Rubric

|               | Verdict           | Evidence from the frames                                                                                              |
| ------------- | ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Storytelling  | Pass              | Problem, ask, answer, proof, share: the opening gives the product something to solve, which the first cut lacked      |
| Art direction | Pass              | One near-black stage with a cobalt and a warm glow in all ten shots; the white interface is the only bright surface   |
| Typography    | Pass, with a note | One line at a time. Note: one typeface throughout, and the statement lines are small against a large dark frame       |
| Composition   | Pass, with a note | Wide shots are turned and fill the frame. Note: shots 5 and 8 still leave a third of the frame as empty stage         |
| Shot variety  | Pass              | Notes, one component, type, a turned wide shot, a flat card, two close-ups, type, brand                               |
| Motion        | Not judged        | Stills cannot show easing or rhythm. The plan gives one camera move per shot; watch the file before trusting this row |
| Transitions   | Pass              | Every cut is a hard cut on an action or a change of idea; the notes are swept away before the product appears         |
| Assets        | Revise to publish | Everything is a placeholder: example product, synthesised effects, and a system voice. No music                       |
| Brand         | Pass              | The stage takes the product's cobalt; the mark is redrawn light for the dark stage                                    |
| Coherence     | Pass              | One stage, one accent, one voice                                                                                      |

## What would still improve it

1. A real voice and music. The narration is the system voice and there is no music bed.
2. A second typeface for the statement lines, so the brand speaks differently from its interface.
3. Shot 5 could sit in the app, like shot 7, instead of floating.
