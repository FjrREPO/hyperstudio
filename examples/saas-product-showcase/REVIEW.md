Answered on 2026-11-19 from the rendered files: a sheet at 1.25 frames a second of each, four full frames, and
the motion strips with their numbers. Sound was measured, not heard. Lumen is an example product; its
interface, mark and sound effects are placeholders, and there is no narration or music.

## What the previous review found, and what changed

| Finding                                                  | Change                                                                                               |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Opening type dim on black, or small in an empty frame    | The opening line is headline-size serif, in frame with the product from the first frame              |
| A line of centred type between shots read as a dead beat | No type-only scenes: every line sits beside the product while it works                               |
| Loading to result looked like a swap of pictures         | One card: the skeleton sits inside it and fades as the answer's parts arrive; the figure counts up   |
| Close-ups cropped the interface                          | Zoom is capped and the product keeps its column; nothing is framed tighter than the answer card      |
| The camera only zoomed and panned                        | Dark cut: the angle and the focus change with the product's state                                    |
| Ending was a logo and tagline on an empty stage          | The interface resolves into its own mark, where it sits in the sidebar; the tagline arrives under it |

## Remaining weaknesses

1. The product is an example. A real interface would do more for "premium" than any change here.
2. No music and no voice; the effects are synthesised.
3. The mark at the end is shown in a chip of sidebar colour, which looks like a badge on the dark stage.
4. The dark cut holds a still frame more than a third of the time.
5. Easing and rhythm at full speed were not judged; that needs a person watching the files.
