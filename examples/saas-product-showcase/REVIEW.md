Placeholders in both: the brand (Lumen is an example product), its interface, the mark, and the sound
effects, which are synthesised. There is no narration and no music.

## What the films do

Both mount the product's own page and drive it. In the light cut one unbroken 9.2 s take types the question,
sends it, shows the product reading, brings the answer in part by part, draws the chart, outlines the row the
answer rests on, and shares it. The camera follows the cursor and never cuts while something is happening.
The dark cut splits the same actions into two takes around one line of type and looks at the interface from
an angle.

## What would still improve them

1. A real product. The interface is an example written for the film, and it shows.
2. Music and a real voice.
3. The endings: both still close on mark, name and tagline over the stage.
4. The light cut's type cards need a stronger composition or should go.
