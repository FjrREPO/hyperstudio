## Decision

- [x] Every row that can pass does, and the film does not read as a template. Rendered.

What would make it better, in order:

1. A real product, with its own brand and typeface.
2. Sound design and music from a real source. The current track is synthesised clicks and tones.
3. A larger cursor, or a closer framing, in shot 3 so the send click reads.
4. One shot with depth (a tilted panel, or two layers) to break the run of flat cards.

This is a floor, not a ceiling. It is a designed, coherent 15 seconds. It is not agency work, and whether it is
good enough to publish for a real product is for a person to judge on that product's film.
